About

A simple, accessible portal for managing everyday academic tasks.

One place for student life

StudentHub brings attendance, results, learning resources, events and support into one responsive interface.

The layout uses CSS Grid for page-level structure and card collections, while Flexbox handles navigation, filters and compact content alignment.
