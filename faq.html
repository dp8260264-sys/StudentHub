Common Questions

How do I check my attendance?

Open Attendance from the StudentHub navigation to view course-wise attendance.

Where can I find study materials?

Open Study Resources to view notes, slides, assignments and past papers.

How do I report an incorrect mark?

Use Support & Complaints to submit an academic request for faculty review.

How do I update my profile?

Open Profile & Settings, update the available fields and select Save changes.
